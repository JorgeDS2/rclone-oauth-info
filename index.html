Personal Google Drive Connector

This is a single-user Google OAuth application for Jorge Davis. It allows rclone and software Jorge controls on his own computer to access his Google Drive after he grants permission in Google's authorization screen.

It is not a public service or a way for other people to access Jorge's files. Google Drive access can include reading and changing files, depending on the actions Jorge requests.

Read the privacy policy for details about the data accessed and how to revoke permission.

Questions about this application can be raised through the repository's issues page.
